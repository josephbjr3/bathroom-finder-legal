# Clean Bathroom Finder Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11-17-2026

This policy explains how Joseph Favor ("we") handles information in the Clean Bathroom Finder mobile app. Contact us at cleanbathroomfinder@gmail.com with questions or requests.

## Information we collect

Location. If you allow location access, the app uses your device location to center the map and find nearby businesses. The map area being searched is sent to Google Maps Platform. We do not save a history of your location to your account.

Account information. When you create an account or sign in with email, Google, or Apple, Firebase Authentication and your chosen sign-in provider process your email address, account identifiers, and authentication data. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a private relay email address. If you sign in with Google, the app may use your Google account's name and profile photo as the starting values for your profile, and that photo may be shown with your reviews until you change or remove it.

Profile. Your first and last name, optional display name, and optional profile photo are stored with your account. Your display name (or name) and profile photo are shown to other users alongside your reviews.

Reviews and photos. Ratings, review text, and photos you submit are stored with Firebase and shown to other users of the app, together with your display name and profile photo. Do not include sensitive personal information in reviews or photos.

Reports and feedback. If you report a review or send feedback, we store your account identifier, the reason and details you provide, and the time it was sent. Reports and feedback are visible only to us, not to other users. We are notified of new reports and feedback by email, sent through Google's Gmail service, and the email includes what you submitted and the reported review.

Moderation. To prevent abuse, we keep a count of how many reports and feedback messages each account sends in a 24-hour period; submissions over the limit are saved but not acted on. When a review receives several reports from different users, it is automatically hidden from other users until we review it. The author can still see their hidden review, marked as hidden, in their profile.

Blocked users. If you block another user, we store that user's account identifier and display name with your account so their reviews are hidden from you. Only you can see your blocked list, and you can unblock users in Settings.

App integrity. Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple DeviceCheck on iOS to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. This processes device and app attestation data.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report: the error and where it happened in the app's code, your device model, operating system version, app version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your reviews, photos, location, or contact details, and are used only to find and fix problems.

## How we use information

We use this information to provide the map and nearby search, operate your account, show reviews and photos, review reports and feedback, protect the service against abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## Service providers

The app uses Google Maps Platform for maps and nearby place search, Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, and Crashlytics) for accounts, data storage, and app services, Gmail to notify us of reports and feedback, and Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple if you choose them. These providers process data on our behalf and under their own terms and privacy policies. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where these providers operate. We use only providers that commit to protecting personal data with safeguards at least as protective as those described in this policy, and we share with them only what they need to provide their service.

The app does not include advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Crash reports are used only for fixing problems.

## Retention

We keep your account, profile, reviews, photos, reports, feedback, and blocked list until you delete them or delete your account. Copies of reports and feedback also remain in our email inbox. You can edit or delete your own reviews at any time. Residual copies may remain in our service providers' backups and logs for a limited period before they are overwritten. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: open the menu, choose Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile and profile photo, your reviews and review photos (including reports made about them), reports and feedback you submitted, your blocked list and report counts, and your sign-in account. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes the app's access to your Apple ID. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in, email cleanbathroomfinder@gmail.com from the address on your account to request deletion. You can also follow the steps at https://josephbjr3.github.io/bathroom-finder-legal/delete-account.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke location access in your device settings; without it, the map may not center on your location. You can sign out from the app menu. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact cleanbathroomfinder@gmail.com to make a request.

## Do Not Track

The app does not track you across third-party websites or apps, so it does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact cleanbathroomfinder@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## Security

We use Firebase security rules, App Check, and authenticated access to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, take reasonable steps to notify you.
