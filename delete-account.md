# Delete Your Clean Bathroom Finder Account

You can delete your Clean Bathroom Finder account and its data at any time.

## Delete in the app

1. Open Clean Bathroom Finder and sign in.
2. Open the menu and choose Settings.
3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If it has been a while since you signed in, the app may ask you to sign out and sign back in first. If you signed in with Apple, you will be asked to confirm with your Apple ID.

## If you can't sign in

Email cleanbathroomfinder@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your sign-in account
- Your profile, including your name and profile photo
- Your reviews and review photos, and any reports made about them
- Reports and feedback you submitted
- Your blocked users list

If you signed in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

## What may be kept

- Copies of reports and feedback in our email inbox, which we delete within 90 days of your request.
- Crash reports, which contain no account details and are automatically deleted after 90 days.
- Copies in our service providers' backups and logs, for a limited time until they are overwritten.

Deletion cannot be undone.
